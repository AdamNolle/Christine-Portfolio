A little about me

We’ll go at the pace you need.

I’ve worked in inpatient psychiatric hospitals, in individual and group therapy, and even inside the insurance industry — which means I’ve seen this work from a lot of angles. What’s stayed constant is the belief that people heal in relationships where they feel safe, heard, and treated with respect.

Some weeks we’ll laugh, some weeks we won’t. Some weeks we’ll just sit and untangle one small thing. Whatever you bring, I’ll meet it with care.

— Christine

How we’ll work together

Steady, kind, and truly yours.

Three pieces I bring to every session — woven together, never rigid, always in service of what you actually need.

I Chapter I

Client-Centered Therapy

You are the expert on your own life. My job is to listen carefully, hold space without judgment, and trust the wisdom you bring into the room.

II Chapter II

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)

When the same thoughts keep looping, CBT gives us a quiet, practical way to step outside them — to notice, question, and try something different.

III Chapter III

A Strengths-Based Lens

I believe you already carry what you need. Together we’ll find those strengths, dust them off, and use them to build the life you actually want.

“My counseling style is warm and non-judgmental. I integrate kindness into every session, and I don’t believe in stigmatizing labels. You’ll be treated with respect, sensitivity, and compassion — every time.”

Christine LCSW · in her own words

How I help

Whatever brings you in,
you’re welcome here.

You don’t need to know which of these fits — only that something feels worth tending. We’ll find the words together.

01

Anxiety

We’ll slow things down together — name what your worry is doing, and find practical ways to soften its grip. You don’t have to keep bracing.

02

Depression

When everything feels heavy, even small things count. We’ll work gently — with kindness, evidence-based tools, and your own strengths leading the way.

03

OCD

I use exposure and response prevention (ERP) and CBT to help you step out of the loop, one careful experiment at a time, at a pace that feels human.

04

Substance Use

Without judgment, we’ll look at what’s underneath — what helps, what hurts, and what you actually want your life to feel like. Recovery is yours to shape.

05

Grief & Loss

Grief doesn’t move in a straight line. I’ll sit with you in it — honoring who and what you’ve lost while we make room for living again.

06

Stress & Burnout

When you’re stretched thin, we’ll look at the load you’re carrying and find what can be set down, redistributed, or simply tended to with more care.

07

Relationships

Family, partners, friendships, work — relationships shape almost everything. We’ll work on what feels stuck, and on what feels worth tending.

And the rest.

If what you’re carrying isn’t on this list, that’s okay. Reach out anyway — I’d rather hear from you than have you wonder.
